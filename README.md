# Better Outdoor Lights Durham

Website for **BOLD (Better Outdoor Lights Durham)**, a volunteer effort by Durham, NC neighbors asking the City to right-size residential streetlights. #BetterOutdoorLightsDurham

Live site: https://davidbradway.github.io/better-outdoor-lights-durham/

## Background

On September 17, 2026, lighting engineer Dane Sanders, PE (Clanton & Associates) measured streetlights in Old West Durham and Watts-Hillandale. Quiet residential intersections measured 4 to 7 times brighter than the IES RP-8-25 national standard, and light falling on homes exceeded the IES maximum by more than 80 times. The site summarizes those findings and asks residents to write to City Council.

## Contents

- `index.html`: the whole site, a single static page with inline styles
- `*.jpg`: site photos from the night walk
- `clanton-night-walk-2026-09-17.pdf`: the full Clanton & Associates report
- `.github/workflows/pages.yml`: deploys the site to GitHub Pages on every push to `main`

## Editing

Edit `index.html` and push to `main`. The workflow publishes the update within a minute or two. To preview locally, open `index.html` in a browser.
